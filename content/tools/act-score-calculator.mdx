## How it works

The enhanced ACT has three required sections and one optional one. Each is scored from **1 to 36**:

| Section | Questions | In the composite? |
|---|---|---|
| English | 50 | Yes |
| Math | 45 | Yes |
| Reading | 36 | Yes |
| Science (optional) | 40 | No |

Your **composite** is the average of English, Math and Reading, rounded to the nearest whole number. Fractions
of one-half or more round up. If you take Science, you also get a **STEM score**: the average of Math and Science.

ACT converts your number correct to a 1–36 score with a separate table for each test form, so the same raw
count can be worth a point more or less on a different form. This calculator uses **our own estimating curve**,
drawn as straight lines between these anchor points, rather than any one form's table:

| Share correct | 0% | 10% | 25% | 50% | 75% | 90% | 100% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Section score (about) | 1 | 8 | 13 | 19 | 26 | 31 | 36 |

Each section estimate is a whole number with a likely range of 1 point either side.

## Worked example

You get **38 of 50** English, **30 of 45** Math and **26 of 36** Reading questions right.

1. English: 38 ÷ 50 = 76% correct, an estimate of about **26**.
2. Math: 30 ÷ 45 = 67% correct, about **24**.
3. Reading: 26 ÷ 36 = 72% correct, about **25**.
4. Composite: (26 + 24 + 25) ÷ 3 = 25 exactly, so **25**.

If you also took Science and got 28 of 40 right (about **25**), your STEM score is (24 + 25) ÷ 2 = 24.5,
which rounds up to **25**.

## When to use this

- After a practice test without its own scoring table.
- To see how much one section is holding your composite back.
- To decide whether to take the optional Science section, or to compare with a
  [digital SAT estimate](/test-prep/sat-score-calculator).

## Common mistakes

- **Adding Science into the composite.** On the enhanced ACT, Science is reported separately.
- **Rounding .5 down.** An average of 30.5 is a composite of 31.
- **Using the old question counts.** The pre-2025 ACT had 75 English and 60 Math questions; those counts no longer apply.
- **Expecting a superscore from this page.** Superscoring combines your best sections across test dates; this calculator estimates one sitting.
